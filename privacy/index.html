Privacy Policy

PixShift — free image tools. Private by design.

Effective: 27 October 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how PixShift (“the App”), published by Builtinsoft, handles information when you use the Android application (package ID com.pixshift.app).

Short version: your photos stay on your phone. PixShift does not upload your images to a server, does not require an account, and does not use ads or tracking SDKs.

1. Who we are

PixShift is a free set of on-device image tools (compress, resize, convert, crop, edit, PDF, collage, and related utilities). Processing happens on your device using the App’s local code and libraries.

2. Information we collect

We do not collect personal information. In particular, PixShift does not:

  • Create user accounts or login profiles
  • Upload, store, or transmit your photos or edited results to our servers
  • Sell or share your data with advertisers or data brokers
  • Use analytics, advertising, or crash-reporting SDKs that identify you

Images you open in the App are read and processed locally. Results are written only when you choose Save, Gallery, or Share, and only to locations you select on your device (or to apps you pick in the Android share sheet).

3. Permissions

Android may show permission prompts. Here is what PixShift uses:

Photos and files Only what you pick The App accesses only images you select through the system picker. It cannot browse your full gallery or other files on its own.
Saving results When you tap Files are saved only after you tap Save, Gallery, or Share. On Android 9 and below, the system may ask for storage permission when saving to the gallery.
Internet Fonts only Network access is used to download the App’s display fonts (Google Fonts). Your pictures are never sent over the network by PixShift.
Camera Not used PixShift does not use the camera.
Microphone Not used PixShift does not use the microphone.
Location Not used PixShift does not access device GPS. If a photo’s EXIF metadata contains location, that data stays inside the file on your device; tools that read or strip EXIF run locally so you can remove it yourself.
Contacts, calls, and SMS Not used PixShift does not access contacts, call logs, or messages.

4. Children’s privacy

The App is not directed at children under 13 (or the equivalent minimum age in your country). We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. Because we do not collect personal data through the App, there is no children’s data for us to delete from our servers.

5. Third-party services

Font files may be fetched from Google Fonts when the App needs them. That request may expose a standard network request (such as IP address) to Google under Google’s own policies. It does not include your photos.

If you use Android’s Share sheet, the destination app (email, messaging, Drive, etc.) is governed by that app’s privacy policy—not PixShift’s.

Distribution through Google Play is subject to Google’s terms and privacy policy for Play.

6. Data retention

Because PixShift does not upload or store your images on our servers, we retain no photo library or account database. Any temporary working files created on your device are managed by the App and the operating system on that device.

7. Security

On-device processing means your images are not transmitted by PixShift to a remote backend. You remain responsible for device lock, backups, and any apps you share files with.

8. International users

PixShift is designed so photo processing does not leave your device. Font downloads may contact Google’s font infrastructure, which can involve servers outside your country.

9. Changes to this policy

We may update this page if the App’s behaviour changes (for example, if a future version adds optional cloud features). The “Effective” date at the top will change when we do. Continued use of the App after an update means you accept the revised policy.
